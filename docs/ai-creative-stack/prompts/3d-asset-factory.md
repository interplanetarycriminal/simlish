# 3D asset factory

Turn approved reference images into validated assets. Choose one image-to-3D model per asset from the brief's hardware and license limits: Stable Fast 3D (about 6 GB VRAM, commercial use under the Stability AI Community License threshold), SAM 3D Objects (32 GB, mask required) or TRELLIS.2 (24 GB, check its bundled models' licenses). Do not run all three. Clean geometry, retopologize only when the budget or deformation needs it, rebuild UVs after retopology, assemble PBR materials with correct color spaces, validate and export. Record model, checkpoint, seed and parameters in the [asset manifest](../templates/asset-manifest.json). Follow the [asset factory workflow](../docs/workflows/3d-asset-factory.md) and the [activation prompt](activate-creative-agent.md).
